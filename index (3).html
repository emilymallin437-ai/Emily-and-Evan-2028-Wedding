Good to Know

What should I wear?

Fancy, smart or cocktail attire. Please avoid white and sage green, as these are reserved for the bride and bridal party. It’s an Irish October, so a layer or wrap is a good idea.

Can I bring a plus one?

Ours is a small wedding of around 70 guests, so we’re keeping numbers close. Please list everyone who will be attending in the RSVP form, and we’ll be looking at final numbers closer to the date.

Are children welcome?

We’re keeping the day adults-only, so unfortunately we’re unable to welcome children. Thank you for understanding.

Will the ceremony be religious?

No, ours is a non-religious ceremony, personal to the two of us.

Gifts

Your presence is the greatest gift. If you would like to give something, we would be very grateful for a gift of money.
